About Me

I'm Stephen! I'm a junior majoring in Information Systems with a minor in Business of Applied AI. I'd say I'm pretty laid-back and easy to get along with, but I'm also really competitive, especially when it comes to things I care about. I can be quiet at first or pretty outgoing depending on who I'm around. Overall, I just like meeting new people, having fun, and being around good company.

A lot of my competitive nature comes from running track in high school. Whether it's video games, board games, trivia, or even a class Kahoot, I'm always playing to win, regardless of whether I've played before. I'm also someone who takes my goals seriously and puts in the effort to achieve them. Outside of school, I enjoy basketball, music, and pretty much anything related to DC or Marvel, from movies and shows to comics.

Chicago Bulls

I spent the early part of my childhood in Chicago's Rogers Park neighborhood before my family eventually moved to the southwest suburbs. With most of my family living in Chicago, we spent a lot of time visiting relatives throughout the city. Whenever we went to my uncle's house, there was a good chance a Bulls game would be on TV. Around 2011, when I was about six years old, I started paying more attention to what was happening on the screen. I remember watching this one player who was constantly flying up and down the court, incredibly explosive and athletic, and everyone around me would go crazy whenever he made a play. That player was Derrick Rose.

Watching Rose play, along with the excitement my family and their friends had for the Bulls, is what originally got me interested in basketball, and I've been a diehard Bulls fan ever since.

Ghanaian Heritage

Both of my parents were born and raised in Ghana, so I've grown up around Ghanaian culture my entire life. One thing I've always appreciated about Ghanaian culture is how welcoming and friendly the community is. There's also a strong emphasis on family, discipline, and respect, which are values that have influenced and shaped who I am today.

One of my favorite parts of Ghanaian culture is definitely the food. I grew up eating dishes like jollof rice, plantains, and waakye at home, so they've always been a part of my life. I've also had the opportunity to visit Ghana a few times, and those experiences have helped me feel more connected to my culture and also given me a better understanding of what life is like in Ghana. There's so much to see and do, and I'm looking forward to going back this December.
